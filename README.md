# JNL Quantum Wireless Call Alarm SMS Notifications

## Opt-In for Resident Call Alarm Alerts

As a valued team member, you have the option to receive resident call alarm notifications from the **JNL Quantum Wireless Call System**, managed by **B&B Integrations**, via SMS text messages.

These notifications may include alerts generated by:

- Wireless nurse call stations
- Resident call buttons
- Other JNL Quantum system alarm events

### What to Expect

- Approximately **5–20 text messages per day**
- Standard **message and data rates may apply**
- Notifications are sent securely through an **encrypted server**

### Privacy & Security

Your phone number will:

- Remain private and confidential
- Be used exclusively for JNL Quantum notification services
- Not be shared, sold, or used by B&B Integrations for any other purpose

### Opt-Out Option

You may stop receiving notifications at any time by replying:

**STOP**

### Consent to Receive SMS Notifications

By replying **YES**, you consent to receive SMS messages related to resident call alarm notifications from the JNL Quantum Wireless Call System.

**Reply YES to opt in.**

Thank you!
