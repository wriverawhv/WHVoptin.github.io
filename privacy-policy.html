Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JNL Quantum Wireless Call System notifications, managed by B&B Integrations, collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through our SMS opt-in process.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you opt in to receive SMS messages:

  • Mobile phone number
  • Consent records showing that you opted in to receive SMS notifications
  • Timestamp and date of opt-in
  • Opt-out or help requests

How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number and related consent information only to send SMS notifications related to resident call alarm notifications, including:

  • Resident call button activations
  • Wireless nurse call station alerts
  • JNL Quantum system alarm notifications

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number for marketing or unrelated purposes. Your number will be used exclusively for JNL Quantum notification services. We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when necessary to deliver SMS notifications or maintain service reliability, and only under confidentiality obligations.

Message and Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply. Your wireless carrier may charge fees for SMS messages, depending on your mobile plan.

Opt-In and Opt-Out

By submitting your phone number and confirming your consent, you agree to receive SMS notifications related to resident call alarm alerts. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message. You may also reply HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

We retain consent and SMS opt-in records only as long as necessary to manage the notification service, comply with legal obligations, and maintain accurate records of your preferences.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or mobile network is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request that we confirm whether we hold your information, update your contact information, or remove your SMS opt-in record, subject to applicable legal requirements and operational needs.
